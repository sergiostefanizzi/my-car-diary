# My Car Diary

A comprehensive vehicle maintenance tracker application designed to help you keep track of your car's maintenance history and upcoming service intervals.

## Features

- **Mileage Tracking**: Monitor the total kilometers traveled and current odometer reading
- **Service Reminders**: Get alerts for scheduled maintenance tasks
- **Maintenance Records**: Keep detailed logs of:
  - Oil changes and fluid top-ups
  - Tire rotations and replacements
  - Regular servicing and inspections
  - Repairs and parts replacements
  - Fuel consumption tracking
- **Maintenance Intervals**: Set custom reminders for:
  - Oil change intervals
  - Tire rotation schedules
  - Inspection intervals
  - Service milestones
- **Expense Tracking**: Monitor spending on maintenance and repairs
- **Service History**: View complete maintenance history with dates and mileage

## Purpose

My Car Diary helps you:
- Keep your vehicle in optimal condition
- Never miss important maintenance tasks
- Plan maintenance costs
- Maintain accurate service records for resale value
- Extend your vehicle's lifespan
